# Interior vs. Exterior Window Cleaning: What Is Included?

Exterior window cleaning focuses on outdoor glass and outdoor buildup. Interior window cleaning addresses fingerprints, smudges, and indoor film. Many customers book both for a complete result.

Optional add-ons can include screen cleaning and track, sill, or frame detailing.
