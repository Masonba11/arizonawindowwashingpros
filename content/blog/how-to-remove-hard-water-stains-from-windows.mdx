# How to Remove Hard Water Stains from Windows

Hard water stains are one of the most frustrating window cleaning challenges in Arizona. The mineral-heavy water leaves behind calcium and magnesium deposits that create unsightly white spots and streaks.

## Why Hard Water Stains Happen in Arizona

Arizona's water supply is extremely hard, containing high levels of dissolved minerals. When this water dries on your windows, it leaves behind mineral deposits that bond to the glass surface. Over time, these deposits become increasingly difficult to remove.

## DIY Methods That Work

### Method 1: White Vinegar Solution
Mix equal parts white vinegar and water. Apply to the stained area and let it sit for 5-10 minutes. Wipe with a microfiber cloth. This works for light to moderate stains.

### Method 2: Baking Soda Paste
Create a paste with baking soda and water. Gently scrub the stained area with a soft cloth. Rinse thoroughly. This is effective for more stubborn deposits.

### Method 3: Commercial Hard Water Removers
Products like CLR or Lime-A-Way can be effective, but must be used carefully to avoid damaging window frames or seals.
