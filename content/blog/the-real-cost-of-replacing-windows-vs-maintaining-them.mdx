# The Real Cost of Replacing Windows vs Maintaining Them

Window replacement costs thousands of dollars. Regular professional cleaning extends window life and prevents costly replacements. This article breaks down the math.

## Window Replacement Costs

- **Single window**: $300-$1,000+
- **Full home replacement**: $5,000-$15,000+
- **Luxury homes**: $10,000-$30,000+
- **Energy-efficient upgrades**: Additional $2,000-$5,000

## Maintenance Costs

- **Professional cleaning**: $150-$350 per service
- **Frequency**: Every 3-4 months
- **Annual cost**: $450-$1,400
- **10-year cost**: $4,500-$14,000

## The Math

Even with regular professional cleaning, maintenance costs are typically 30-50% less than replacement costs over 10 years. Plus, maintenance:
- Preserves window condition
- Maintains energy efficiency
- Prevents seal failure
- Protects home value

## When Replacement Becomes Necessary

Replacement is needed when:
- Windows are permanently etched
- Seals have failed
- Frames are damaged
- Energy efficiency is compromised

## Prevention Through Maintenance

Regular professional cleaning:
- Prevents hard water etching
- Identifies issues early
- Extends window lifespan
- Maintains appearance and value
