# Why Arizona New Builds Get Dust Buildup Faster

New construction in Arizona creates more dust and debris. Learn why new builds need more frequent window cleaning and how to protect your investment.

## Why New Builds Get Dustier

- **Construction debris**: Ongoing development nearby
- **Landscaping**: New plants and soil create dust
- **Unpaved areas**: Dirt roads and construction zones
- **Wind patterns**: Open areas without mature trees
- **Settling materials**: New construction materials create particles

## Impact on Windows

- **Faster buildup**: Windows get dirty 2-3x faster
- **Abrasive particles**: Construction dust can scratch glass
- **Hard water combination**: Dust + hard water = worse stains
- **Visibility issues**: Reduced clarity affects home enjoyment

## Protection Strategies

1. **More frequent cleaning**: Every 2-3 months for new builds
2. **Professional service**: Deionized water prevents damage
3. **Early maintenance**: Start cleaning immediately after move-in
4. **Protective measures**: Consider window protection during construction
