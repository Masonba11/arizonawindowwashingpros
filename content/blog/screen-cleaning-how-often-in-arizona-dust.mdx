# Screen Cleaning: How Often in Arizona Dust?

Arizona's dust storms and pollen require more frequent screen cleaning. Learn the optimal schedule and why clean screens improve air quality and visibility.

## Why Screens Get Dirty Faster in Arizona

- **Dust storms**: Frequent haboobs deposit heavy dust
- **Pollen**: High pollen counts during spring
- **Construction**: Ongoing development creates particles
- **Low humidity**: Dust stays on screens longer
- **Wind patterns**: Carries dust from surrounding areas

## Recommended Cleaning Schedule

- **Standard homes**: Every 3-4 months
- **High dust areas**: Every 2-3 months
- **Allergy sufferers**: Every 2-3 months
- **New construction**: Every 2 months during construction

## Benefits of Clean Screens

- **Better air quality**: Removes allergens and particles
- **Improved visibility**: Clear screens = better views
- **Extended screen life**: Prevents damage from buildup
- **Energy efficiency**: Clean screens don't block airflow
