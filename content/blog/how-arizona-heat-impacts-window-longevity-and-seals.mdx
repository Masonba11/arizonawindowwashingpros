# How Arizona Heat Impacts Window Longevity and Seals

Arizona's extreme heat accelerates window seal failure and frame degradation. Regular cleaning helps identify issues early and extends window life.

## Heat's Impact on Windows

### Seal Failure
- **Thermal expansion**: Heat causes seals to expand and contract
- **UV degradation**: Sunlight breaks down seal materials
- **Premature failure**: Seals fail 2-3x faster in Arizona
- **Energy loss**: Failed seals reduce insulation

### Frame Damage
- **Vinyl warping**: Extreme heat can warp vinyl frames
- **Aluminum expansion**: Metal frames expand in heat
- **Wood deterioration**: Sun and heat damage wood frames
- **Paint fading**: UV radiation fades frame finishes

## Early Detection Through Cleaning

Regular professional cleaning allows us to:
- **Inspect seals**: Identify early signs of failure
- **Check frames**: Spot warping or damage
- **Assess condition**: Recommend repairs before replacement
- **Extend lifespan**: Maintenance prevents premature failure

## Prevention Strategies

1. **Regular cleaning**: Every 3-4 months
2. **Professional inspection**: During cleaning service
3. **Early repairs**: Fix issues before they worsen
4. **Quality windows**: Invest in heat-resistant materials
