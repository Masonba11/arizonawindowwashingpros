# How Often Should You Clean Your Windows in Northern Arizona?

There is no single schedule that fits every home. Northern Arizona properties vary between full-time residences, seasonal cabins, and vacation rentals. Many homeowners clean exterior glass seasonally, before guests arrive, or when windows start looking dull in morning or afternoon light.

Homes that sit unused for weeks can collect outdoor dust on glass and screens. Properties with frequent door and window use may need interior attention more often.
