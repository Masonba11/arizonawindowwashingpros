# How Often Should You Clean Windows in Gilbert, AZ?

Gilbert's desert climate requires more frequent window cleaning than many other areas. This guide explains the optimal cleaning schedule for Gilbert homes.

## Factors Affecting Cleaning Frequency

- **Dust storms**: More frequent during monsoon season
- **Hard water**: Mineral deposits build up quickly
- **Sun exposure**: Intense UV accelerates damage
- **Home location**: Proximity to construction or agriculture
- **Window type**: Single vs. double-pane affects maintenance needs

## Recommended Schedule

- **Standard homes**: Every 3-4 months
- **New construction areas**: Every 2-3 months
- **Luxury homes**: Every 2-3 months for optimal appearance
- **Homes near agriculture**: Every 2-3 months during growing season
