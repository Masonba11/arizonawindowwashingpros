# Why Deionized Water Matters for Window Cleaning

In Arizona, traditional window cleaning methods often leave behind streaks and spots. The solution? Deionized water.

## The Problem with Arizona Water

Arizona's water supply contains extremely high levels of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium. When this water is used for window cleaning and then dries, it leaves behind visible mineral deposits. These deposits create:

- White spots and streaks
- Cloudy appearance
- Reduced visibility
- Hard-to-remove buildup over time

## What is Deionized Water?

Deionized water is water that has had all mineral ions removed through a specialized filtration process. This creates ultra-pure water that contains zero dissolved minerals.

## Benefits of Deionized Water for Window Cleaning

### 1. No Streaks or Spots
Because there are no minerals in the water, it dries completely clear with no residue left behind.

### 2. Prevents Future Buildup
Without minerals, there's nothing to deposit on your windows, reducing the need for frequent cleaning.

### 3. Better Results
Your windows will have a crystal-clear, polished appearance that lasts longer.

### 4. Safe for All Surfaces
Deionized water is safe for windows, frames, and surrounding surfaces.
