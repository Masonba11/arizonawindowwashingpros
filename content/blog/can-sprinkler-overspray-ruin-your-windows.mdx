# Can Sprinkler Overspray Ruin Your Windows?

Arizona's hard water in sprinkler systems can leave mineral deposits on windows when overspray occurs. This article explains how to prevent and remove sprinkler overspray damage.

## Understanding Sprinkler Overspray Damage

When sprinkler systems spray hard water onto windows, the mineral-rich water dries and leaves behind calcium and magnesium deposits. These deposits can:

- Create white spots and streaks
- Reduce window clarity
- Lead to permanent etching if untreated
- Damage window frames and seals

## Prevention Strategies

1. **Adjust sprinkler heads** to avoid window overspray
2. **Install window protection** during irrigation
3. **Use soft water** for irrigation systems (if possible)
4. **Regular professional cleaning** to remove deposits before they etch

## Professional Removal

If your windows already have sprinkler overspray damage, [professional window cleaning](/services/exterior-window-cleaning) with deionized water can remove deposits and restore clarity.
