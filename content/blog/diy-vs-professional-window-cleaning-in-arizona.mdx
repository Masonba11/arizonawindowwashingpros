# DIY vs Professional Window Cleaning in Arizona

Arizona's hard water and intense sun make DIY window cleaning challenging. This comparison helps you decide between DIY and professional service.

## DIY Challenges in Arizona

- **Hard water**: Leaves streaks and spots
- **Heat**: Water evaporates too quickly
- **Safety**: Multi-story homes require ladders
- **Time**: Takes hours for thorough cleaning
- **Results**: Often disappointing despite effort

## Professional Advantages

- **Deionized water**: Zero streaks or spots
- **Experience**: Knows Arizona-specific challenges
- **Equipment**: Professional tools and safety gear
- **Time**: Complete job in hours, not days
- **Guarantee**: Streak-free results guaranteed

## Cost Comparison

While DIY seems cheaper, consider:
- **Time value**: Your time has value
- **Quality**: Professional results last longer
- **Damage prevention**: Avoids costly mistakes
- **Frequency**: Professionals need fewer cleanings

## When to Choose Professional

Choose professional service if you have:
- Multi-story homes
- Hard water stains
- Limited time
- Desire for perfect results
- Investment in home appearance
