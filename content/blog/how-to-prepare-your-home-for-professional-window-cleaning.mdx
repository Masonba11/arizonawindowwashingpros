# How to Prepare Your Home for Professional Window Cleaning

Simple preparation helps appointments go smoothly. Clear access to windows when possible, unlock gates if needed, and note any fragile items near interior panes. For exterior-only work, access instructions may be enough when you cannot be home.

Share pets, multi-story glass, or preferred arrival times when you [request a quote](/contact). For a full home clean, review our [residential window cleaning](/services/residential-window-cleaning) options first.
